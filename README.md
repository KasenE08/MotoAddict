# MotoAddict

Retro pixel idle game: build a motorcycle empire from a Honda Ruckus and a tire-patch stand, then dominate the drag strip on real bikes from scooters to the Ninja H2R.

## Play
Live at **https://kasene08.github.io/MotoAddict/** once GitHub Pages is on.
On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen** for full-screen, offline play.

## Loop
- **SHOP** – tap jobs to earn, buy levels, hire mechanics to automate (they earn offline, 2h cap). Speed x2 at LV 25/50/100/200/300/400, profit x3 every 100 after.
- **RACE** – drag race mini-game. Wait for green, launch in the green zone, shift in the green zone. Beat 3 opponents per league. Leagues go on forever (Infinity).
- **GARAGE** – 21 real bikes. Each owned = +10% income. Tune exhaust, ECU, intake, tires, weight, quickshifter, and final drive.
- **LEGACY** – Rebuild (prestige) for Reputation: +2% income each, or spend it on permanent upgrades.
- **STATS** – 40 achievements (+1% income each), settings, save export/import.
- Random events: rich customers, tips, turbo wrench, parts sales, sponsors, and jammed lifts.
